SONORSite Rig

The site rig, built from this page

A Raspberry Pi that lives in the van. Plug its Ethernet into the site switch, join its Wi-Fi on the tablet, and the tablet gets the Network Scanner and Remotes running on the site LAN, with no laptop. Everything below, from parts to the one-line install, is on this page.

For another VLAN, plug into that VLAN's port, or add its subnet in the Scanner's box. The rig never routes the site LAN to the hotspot's clients except for internet.

6Pair the tablet

  1. On the tablet, join the rig's Wi-Fi (name and password from the label). Android will say "no internet" if the site has none: choose Stay connected.
  2. Open Site Tools, then the Site Rig bookmark, or go to http://10.77.0.1.
  3. The rig page shows the site LAN address, internet, and a tile for each tool. Tap Network Scanner or Remotes.

7Test it

  1. Rig page: Site LAN shows an address (green). If it says "not plugged in", check the cable and the switch port.
  2. Network Scanner → Scan network: the router and switch appear within a minute; the glance bar shows WAN, DNS and DHCP.
  3. Remotes → Find devices: TVs and receivers on the LAN appear as cards.
  4. Rig page → Update the rig: comes back in about two minutes on the same version or newer.

!Limits

  • One LAN at a time. The rig sees the VLAN its cable is on. Other subnets are reachable only if the site routes to them.
  • Hotspot range is a room or two (the Pi's antenna). Keep the rig near where you're working, or use the site's Wi-Fi and the rig's LAN address instead.
  • The rogue-DHCP check and full MAC addresses work on the rig (it has the rights a tablet doesn't).
  • Downloads come from GitHub. First install and updates need internet; once installed it works with none.
  • Passwords: the hotspot password lives in /etc/sonor-rig/config.json on the rig. Change it there and re-run the installer to apply.
